# iPhone launch page

`/iphone18/` compares iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. Each model links to a prefilled WhatsApp inquiry at the existing Connections Punda number. The page takes no payments and makes no local stock or price claims.

## Content sources

Checked September 10, 2026:

- https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/
- https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/
- https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/
- https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/

September 18 (Pro models) and October 23 (Duo) are Apple's initial-market launch dates, not promised Connections arrival dates. Confirm local pricing, available regional models, storage, SIM options, stock and ordering terms before adding purchase or preorder actions.
