Before a phone repair: a little preparation helps.

A clear description of the problem helps the repair team get started. Before you visit, gather the device details and protect the information you want to keep.

By Connections Curaçao · Updated 8 November 2026

In this guide

The starting point

Send the model and symptoms first. Confirm the diagnosis, price and expected timing before authorizing a repair.

Describe what happens, and when

Tell us the model and explain the problem in your own words. Does charging stop when the cable moves? Is the screen damaged, unresponsive or both? Does a sound problem happen during calls or when playing music?

Mention when it began and any relevant drop, liquid exposure or previous repair. A photo can help explain visible damage. Keep passwords, private messages and account details out of photos you send.

Back up what matters

If the device still works, back up important photos, contacts and other information before service. Check that you can access the backup. A repair should never be your only plan for preserving data.

Apple’s service preparation guide includes backup and account-protection steps. Never share your Apple Account password or account security details. If you need to unlock a device for a check, do it yourself and stay present.

If your phone cannot turn on, tell the team. Explain which preparation steps you could not complete and discuss your data concerns before work starts.

Get the diagnosis and quote

Connections offers a free diagnosis and quote, and walk-ins are welcome. Our repair page covers enquiries for screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras and other phone issues.

Ask whether parts are available for your specific model, what the proposed repair includes, the expected completion time and the applicable warranty. Timing depends on the issue and parts required; confirm it with the team.

Bring the useful extras

If the problem involves charging, bring the charger and cable involved. Keep your receipt if you have one and make a note of the symptoms so you do not have to remember them at the counter.

When collecting the device, ask to check the repaired function and clarify any care instructions. Keep the repair record for future reference.
