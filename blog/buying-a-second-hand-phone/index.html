Buying second hand? Make these checks first.

A pre-owned phone deserves a closer look. Compare the individual device, its condition and the terms of the sale—not just the name on the box.

By Connections Curaçao · Updated 8 November 2026

The starting point

Ask about the specific device you are buying. Two phones with the same model name can have very different histories.

Ask for the details that matter

Request the exact model, storage capacity, condition and included accessories. Ask what has been checked, whether any repairs are known, and what warranty or return terms apply to that item. Keep those details with your receipt.

If you are comparing several listings, make a short note for each one. A lower price is easier to assess when you know what is included and what you might need to buy separately.

Check access, battery and everyday functions

For an iPhone, Apple advises checking Activation Lock, carrier restrictions and battery health. Avoid purchasing a device that still requires a previous owner’s account to activate. Where available, review Parts and Service History in Settings.

Test the display, cameras, buttons, speaker, microphone, Wi-Fi and charging. Look for visible damage and ask about anything you do not understand. Battery health is one part of the picture; consider the condition of the whole device.

Apple explains these checks in its pre-owned iPhone guide. For other brands, ask for the equivalent account, battery and hardware checks.

Make sure it works with your number

Confirm the exact SIM or eSIM arrangement and any carrier restriction before paying. If you are keeping an existing Curaçao number, check compatibility with your mobile provider. Do this for the specific device you are buying.

Confirm availability before you visit

Pre-owned inventory can change quickly. Use the Connections second-hand store to see the current listings, then ask about the item that interests you.

A useful message includes the listing, your preferred storage and any questions about condition or accessories. Our team can confirm the details so you know what to expect before making the trip.
