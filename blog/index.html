Good to know

A few quick answers.

Need something specific?
Ask our team on WhatsApp

Where can I check current prices and stock?

Browse the current collection and confirm the exact model, price and availability with our team before visiting. Gift-card prices and stock are shown in the digital store.

Can I ask for a repair quote?

Yes. Send your model and a description of the issue through WhatsApp. Connections offers a free diagnosis and quote, and walk-ins are welcome. Explore repair services.

How do I pay with Suave?

Choose your product, confirm stock and review the application and payment plan in Suave. Online QR orders include a 3% store fee. Approval and final terms are determined by Suave. Read the payment guide.

Where are your stores?

Find us at Ruyterkade 28 in Punda and Bredestraat 12 in Otrobanda. The website lists Monday–Saturday, 9:30am–6:00pm, with Sunday closed. Confirm holiday hours before visiting. See the local guide.
