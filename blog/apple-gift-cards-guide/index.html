Apple gift cards: check the region before you buy.

Buying a digital gift card starts with matching the card to the recipient’s account. Check the region and intended use before choosing an amount.

By Connections Curaçao · Updated 8 November 2026

In this guide

The starting point

Connections lists US Apple gift cards. Confirm that the recipient is eligible to redeem a US card under Apple’s country and account requirements before paying.

Confirm the country and card type

Apple gift cards have country or region restrictions. Apple explains that a card cannot be redeemed outside its country or region of purchase in its redemption help.

Do not assume that every Apple card works with every Apple Account. Check the recipient’s account region and Apple’s current requirements. If you are unsure, resolve that with Apple before buying; changing account details is not a guaranteed solution.

Separate the card value from the price

Read both the digital value you will receive and the checkout price. A US-dollar card denomination and its local selling price are different things. Confirm the currency shown for each.

Use the Connections digital gift-card store for current prices and availability. The catalogue can change, so check the live listing rather than relying on an old screenshot or message.

Follow the checkout and delivery instructions

The Connections website directs Apple gift-card purchases to its digital store. Review the selected card, payment instructions and contact details before confirming an order.

The storefront describes Sentoo payment and WhatsApp delivery after verification. Keep your order reference and use the store’s contact options if you need an update. A payment submission and a verified order are separate steps, so follow the status instructions shown for your purchase.

Keep the code for the intended recipient

Check who will receive the card before ordering a gift. Share the code privately with that person and keep it out of public screenshots.

If you need help, send the order reference and a description of the issue to the store. For redemption errors, follow Apple’s help instructions and avoid repeatedly purchasing another card before understanding the problem.
