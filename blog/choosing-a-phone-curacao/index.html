Phones · 2 min read

Your next phone starts with the right questions.

A phone should fit the way you live. Before comparing model names, think about the things you do every day—and what you wish your current phone did better.

By Connections Curaçao · Updated 8 November 2026

The starting point

Bring your current phone, your preferred budget and a short list of must-haves. That makes comparing your options much easier.

Start with your everyday

Write down your three most important uses. Maybe it is WhatsApp and calls, taking photos of the family, or managing your business on the go. Then name the thing that frustrates you most: running out of storage, a screen that feels too small, or a battery that needs constant attention.

Use that list when comparing devices. Ask to hold the phone, try the keyboard and look at the screen. A comfortable size matters long after the excitement of a new purchase.

Check storage before choosing a capacity

Look at how much space your current phone uses. Allow room for new photos, videos, apps and downloads. If you keep everything on the device, tell our team; if you use cloud storage, include any ongoing subscription in your planning.

Confirm the exact capacity on the listing and receipt. Similar-looking phones can have different storage options and prices.

Confirm the exact model and SIM setup

Ask whether the specific device uses a physical SIM, eSIM, or both. Check with your mobile provider that it supports the model and setup you intend to use in Curaçao. This is especially useful if you have two numbers or travel often.

If you are moving between iPhone and Android, make a list of the apps and accounts you need to bring across. Check that you can sign in to them before changing devices.

Compare the complete purchase

  • Confirm whether the device is new or pre-owned, its condition and what comes in the box.
  • Ask about the warranty for that exact product.
  • Include a suitable charger, cable, case and screen protector if you need them.
  • Confirm the current price and availability before visiting.
