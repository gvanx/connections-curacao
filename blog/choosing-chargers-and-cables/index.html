Accessories · 2 min read

The right charger. The right cable. Less guessing.

A connector that fits is only the first check. Bring your device model and the accessories you already use so you can choose a charging setup that works together.

By Connections Curaçao · Updated 8 November 2026

The starting point

Tell us what you want to charge, how many devices you connect at once and whether the charger will be used at home or while travelling.

Start with the device, then the connector

Check the charging port on your device and both ends of the cable. A charger, cable and device need to work as a complete set. A cable for an older phone may not be the right one for your next model.

When asking for help, send the exact phone or laptop model. A clear photo of the existing charger’s label can also help us identify the setup you are replacing.

Check the charging requirements

Fast-charging requirements vary by model. Apple’s iPhone fast-charging guide specifies the cable and adapter requirements for its devices. Use the manufacturer’s guidance for your exact phone, tablet or laptop.

Ask about supported charging standards, cable capability and the output available from the port you plan to use. Do not rely only on the largest wattage printed on the packaging.

Plan for more than one device

If you want to charge a phone and laptop together, ask how the charger shares its output when multiple ports are in use. Check the manufacturer’s specification for that combination.

Also think about cable length, desk space and what you will carry in a bag. A tidy setup starts with knowing where the charger will live. For travel, check plug compatibility and the charger’s input-voltage label for your destination.

Bring the accessories you want to match

At Connections, ask about currently available charging accessories, including Anker options. We can compare your device requirements with the product information before you buy.

  • Share the exact device model.
  • Say whether you need a charger, cable or both.
  • List the devices you want to charge together.
  • Confirm the price, included items and warranty.

Message our team before visiting if you need a particular connector or charging setup.
