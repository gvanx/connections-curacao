Paying with Suave at Connections: how it works.

Connections handles your product and availability. Suave handles your application and payment plan. Here is what to check before authorizing a purchase.

By Connections Curaçao · Updated 8 November 2026

In this guide

A question of your own?Ask our team

The starting point

Review the final total and Suave payment schedule before you approve. A calculator estimate is not an approval or a final offer.

Choose your product and confirm the details

Find the item you want and confirm the exact model, condition, price and availability with Connections. The purchase depends on stock confirmation as well as Suave approval.

The Connections Suave page states that applicants must be 18 or older with valid identification. Eligibility and available plans are determined by Suave.

Understand the online QR process

For online orders, select Suave at checkout and send the request through WhatsApp. Connections sends a QR code for the checkout amount including the 3% store fee. Scan the code in Suave and review the information presented there.

A simple fee example

If the amount before the online store fee is XCG 1,000, the 3% fee is XCG 30, making the QR amount XCG 1,030. This example is not a monthly-payment quote and does not describe any separate Suave plan charges.

Orders remain pending until payment is verified. Confirm the final amount with the team before authorizing it.

Use the calculator as an estimate

The Connections Suave page includes a calculator and the store’s purchase terms. For an online QR order, its instructions say to enter the total including the 3% store fee.

Check the actual plan, due dates, fees and approval in Suave before agreeing. Available terms may vary by purchase. The store’s terms allow an in-store service fee of up to 3%, disclosed before authorization; ask for the total that applies to your order.

Know where to ask for help

Ask Connections about the product, stock, fulfilment and merchandise questions. Ask Suave about approval, the payment schedule and account adjustments.

Read the current Connections terms and official Suave terms before purchase. Confirm the receipt, warranty and any exchange conditions for your item, and keep your payment records.
