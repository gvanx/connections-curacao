Send the product or repair details before visiting. Confirm stock, the right branch and any special opening hours with our team.

Open directions to Otrobanda

Check the hours before heading out

The website lists both stores as open Monday to Saturday, 9:30am–6:00pm, and closed on Sunday. Contact the store to confirm holiday hours or other schedule changes.

If you need a particular product, colour or storage size, ask about availability and the branch to visit. A listing alone does not confirm that an item is waiting at both locations.

Make your visit count

  • For a new device: bring your preferred budget, essential features and the model you are comparing.
  • For accessories: bring the device or exact model name, plus the cable or charger you want to match.
  • For a repair: describe the symptoms and bring relevant accessories. Back up your information first if you can.
  • For an existing order: keep the order reference or receipt handy.

You can also browse the online collection, read about repairs or explore Suave payments before coming in.
